WebGL troubleshooting

If the WebGL version does not load on Namecheap, rebuild Unity WebGL with Decompression Fallback enabled or set Compression Format to Disabled, then upload the new build again. On the next rebuild, set company name to Josh Barnett (not DefaultCompany) and product label away from WebGL-Staging.
