Privacy Policy

How Castle Keeper handles information. Written for the cozy kingdom prototype and this website.

Last updated: October 24, 2026 · Developer: Josh Barnett

Summary

Castle Keeper is an indie game and website by Josh Barnett. The browser prototype is a Unity WebGL build hosted on this site. We aim to collect as little personal information as possible.

What this policy covers

  • The website at castlekeeper.org
  • The browser playable build at Play Castle Keeper
  • Future desktop builds distributed from this site

Information we collect

Website hosting logs

Like most sites, our hosting provider (Namecheap / LiteSpeed) may automatically record standard server logs such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, and timestamps. These logs are used for security, abuse prevention, and keeping the site online. We do not use them to build marketing profiles.

Game data on your device

Castle Keeper may store progress, settings, and chronicle-style game state locally in your browser (for WebGL) or on your device (for future desktop builds). That data stays on your machine unless you choose to share a screenshot, save file, or similar with us.

Email you send us

If you contact support, we receive whatever you include in your message (name, email address, and the content of your note). We use that only to reply and resolve your request.

What we do not currently do

  • We do not require an account to play the browser prototype.
  • We do not knowingly sell personal information.
  • We do not run third-party advertising networks on this site as of the date above.
  • We do not ask children under 13 to provide personal information.

Cookies and similar technologies

The site may use essential cookies or local storage needed for the game and basic site function (for example remembering a mute preference once that feature ships). We do not presently use non-essential tracking cookies for ads.

Third parties

Hosting and domain services are provided by Namecheap. Unity’s WebGL runtime runs in your browser as part of the playable build. Those providers process technical data under their own policies as needed to deliver the service.

Children

Castle Keeper is a cozy family-friendly kingdom game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Your choices

  • Clear site data / local storage in your browser to remove local WebGL save or setting data.
  • Email us to ask what contact information we hold from your support messages, or to request deletion of that correspondence where feasible.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way (for example when accounts, analytics, or cloud saves are added), we will update the “Last updated” date on this page.
