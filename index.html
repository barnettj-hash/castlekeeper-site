Features

Build, protect, and remember

Mini Living Kingdom

Villagers walk between homes, farms, markets, taverns, and the castle while the kingdom changes around them.

5-Minute Bell Events

The castle bell brings travelers, warnings, opportunities, and decisions that shape the kingdom.

Travelers And Decisions

Welcome merchants, scholars, bards, refugees, knights, inventors, doctors, and mysterious strangers.

Seasons And Weather

Spring flowers, summer bustle, fall harvests, winter snow, rain, fog, and storms change the mood.

Heroes And Diplomacy

Heroes can join the kingdom, neighboring realms remember your choices, and alliances can matter.

Invading Knights

Prepare defenses, guard towers, walls, and diplomacy before hostile forces test the gate.

Rare Dragon Attacks

Major dragon events are rare, dangerous, and meant to become stories in the kingdom history.

Royal Chronicle

Important choices, battles, ruler milestones, disasters, and victories become dated entries.
