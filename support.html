Support

Need a hand with the browser prototype, a blank download button, or something in the kingdom? Start here.

Play in your browser

Use a current desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari) with hardware acceleration enabled. The kingdom view is desktop-first; phone browsers may struggle with WebGL.

Open Play →

WebGL will not load

Refresh once, then try another browser. If it still fails, the Unity build may need Decompression Fallback (or compression disabled) and a re-upload. Include your OS and browser version when you write in.

Windows / Mac downloads

Desktop packages are not public yet. Play in the browser until real builds are uploaded.

Saves and progress

Browser progress is stored locally. Clearing site data can wipe a run. When desktop builds ship, save locations will be listed on this page.
